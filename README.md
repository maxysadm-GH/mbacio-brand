## Brand colors

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Blue | `#1a56db` | CTAs, links, primary buttons |
| Dark Navy | `#1e3a5f` | Headers, backgrounds, footer |
| Accent Teal | `#0ea5e9` | Highlights, secondary accents |
| White | `#ffffff` | Backgrounds, inverse text |
| Dark Text | `#1f2937` | Body copy |

## Usage rights

Logos + ad templates are provided for:

- Third-party articles, tutorials, or partnerships that reference MBACIO
- Integration partners and vendors that need to show the MBACIO logo alongside their own
- Embedding in other MBACIO-authored open-source projects (e.g., [`claude-code-lane-lock`](https://github.com/maxysadm-GH/claude-code-lane-lock))

Please do NOT:

- Modify the logos' colors, proportions, or visual identity without permission
- Imply partnership or endorsement that doesn't exist
- Use the logos in a way that could be confused with MBACIO as the source of a product
- Redistribute these assets as if they were your own brand

For commercial licensing, co-marketing, or questions, contact: **max@mbacio.com**
