## Do

- Use the primary logo wherever possible
- Preserve the logo's aspect ratio and clear space
- Match brand colors exactly (hex values above)
- Use "MBACIO" in all caps when referencing the company
- Spell out the pronunciation in voice contexts

## Do NOT

- Modify the logo's colors or add effects without written permission
- Use the logos to imply partnership or endorsement that doesn't exist
- Say "Databox" — use "Smart Dashboards" in client-facing content
- Use "CIO Agreement" — the canonical term is "MSA" (Master Service Agreement)
- Use the logos in a way that confuses MBACIO as the source of a product you built
